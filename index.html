How to become unfucked

Move your week towards the work that runs on trust. Nobody benchmarks it because it can't be done from a prompt.

Deliver › Explain › Advise › Trusted

  1. Be in the room where the question is formed.AI answers questions well. It doesn't know which question the CFO is actually worried about. Get to the planning conversation, not just the brief that comes out of it. Ask what the board is nervous about this year.
  2. Own the relationship, not the deliverable.Be the named person they call first. Check in when there's nothing to bill: a two-line note on a Finance Act change that affects them is worth more than another deck.
  3. Earn trust on purpose.Trust is built from small promises kept. Reply when you said you would, say "I don't know yet" early, and flag your own mistakes before anyone else finds them. It compounds, and no model can borrow yours.
  4. Translate, don't transcribe.Anyone can generate the analysis now. The value is saying what it means for their cash, their systems and their audit committee, in their language, in one page.
  5. Be the person who signs it off.Judgement with your name on it is the scarce part. Get good at catching the plausible-but-wrong answer. On today's benchmarks that is still half the output.
  6. Run the agents instead of competing with them.Design the workflow, check the output, own the result. In tax technology that's the job title. Every hour the tools save is an hour for points 1 to 4.
  7. Go where the training data isn't.Irish practice, Revenue relationships, how a client's ERP actually behaves, what was agreed on the last call. Local, unwritten and specific knowledge is the moat.
  8. Stay close through implementation.Advice that gets implemented builds the next engagement. Be there when the e-invoicing go-live wobbles, not just when the design is signed.

What's measured

Switch a benchmark off and its task falls back to unmeasured. Several on one task are averaged.

What a benchmark isA fixed set of tasks with known good answers. Every model sits the same exam, so scores can be compared over time.
Why agentic ones matterOlder tests ask one question and mark one answer. The newer ones give a model tools, files and hours, and mark the finished work. That is much closer to a real engagement.
How to read the cubesEach cube fills pink to the top score. Elo benchmarks stay blue: they rank models against each other and have no 100% to fill to.

Before you quote it

  • Everything here is American.No benchmark tests Irish corporation tax, VAT or Revenue practice. That buffer thins every time a model sees more local material.
  • Zero means unmeasured, not safe.Advice, projects, implementation and tool-building score nothing only because nobody tests them.
  • Models, not products.A model inside a good workflow beats a bare prompt, so deployed tools can do better than these numbers.
  • The future is a what-if.Only the trend-holds rate is measured; the other scenarios are scaled from it. Whichever you pick is applied to every benchmark at once.
  • Elo is relative.AA-Briefcase and GDPval-AA only rank models against each other, so they inform but never score.
  • The role mixes are guesses.Estimated for a tax technology and transformation team, not taken from timesheets. Set the sliders to your real week.
